The employee may repay all or part of the outstanding balance earlier than scheduled. Any additional or early payment will reduce the outstanding balance. Maluti Fruit will adjust or stop payroll deductions once the debt has been fully repaid.

7. TERMINATION OF EMPLOYMENT

If my employment with Maluti Fruit ends for any reason before this loan/advance is fully repaid, the outstanding balance will remain a debt owed by me to Maluti Fruit.

I authorise Maluti Fruit, subject to applicable South African law, to deduct any amount that may lawfully be deducted from remuneration or other amounts payable to me when my employment ends.

If the full balance cannot lawfully be deducted from amounts payable to me, the remaining balance will remain payable. The parties may record a lawful repayment arrangement in writing. This clause does not authorise a deduction prohibited by law.
